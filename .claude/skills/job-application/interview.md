# Interview preparation

## Prep pack (`private/applications/<id>/interview_prep_<stage>.md`)

Build it from the archive, not from memory: `posting.md` (what they asked for), the CV and cover
letter actually sent, `outcome.md` (earlier rounds and feedback) and `private/research/<company>.md`.

1. **The role in one paragraph** and the 4-6 things they will test, ranked.
2. **Company briefing**: what they do, recent news (dated, with source), products/teams relevant to the role. Verify-before-use: only facts found on the company's own site or reputable news, each with its source link.
3. **Interviewers** (if named by the person): public professional background only; no personal social media.
4. **Likely questions**, grouped (motivation, technical, behavioural, situational), each mapped to a STAR story from the profile's `star_stories` or from experience bullets.
5. **Gap answers**: for each gap from the evaluation, a short honest answer and the bridge. Never invented experience.
6. **Technical refresh**: concepts from the posting to revise, with a 1-line explanation each.
7. **Questions to ask them**: 5, specific to the role and company.
8. **Logistics**: time, format, who, what to bring; the person fills this in.

Stage focus: screening call = motivation, logistics, salary range; technical = depth on the core tools,
a live task or case; final/panel = impact stories, team fit, questions about the team's priorities.

## STAR stories

Situation - Task - Action - Result, 60-90 seconds spoken. Results with real numbers from the profile
only. If a story lacks a result, help the person recall one; do not supply one.

## Mock interview

Offer it after the pack. One question at a time; wait for the answer; give brief feedback (what landed,
what was missing, a tighter version using only their words and facts), then the next question. Mix
3-4 question types for the stage. End with a short summary of strengths and two things to practise.

How it works

Where the jobs come from

Every day a scheduled GitHub Actions job reads the public job boards that employers publish through their hiring systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and SmartRecruiters). These feeds exist so that anyone can list a company's openings. No site is scraped, nothing logs in, and LinkedIn and Indeed are not touched, because their terms forbid automated access. Only jobs in Ireland, or remote jobs open to Ireland, Europe or EMEA, are kept.

Is the employer genuine?

  • Verified employer: the advert is on the company's own website, or the company's careers page links to the job board it came from.
  • Official job board: the advert is on the company's hiring-system board, but the link from its website could not be confirmed automatically (many careers pages load jobs with scripts).
  • Unverified: found through a job aggregator. Check the employer on its own website before applying.
  • Caution: the advert shows scam warning signs, such as asking for a fee, contact only by WhatsApp or Telegram, or bank, PPS or passport details before an offer. Hidden by default.

No automatic check is perfect. Never pay to apply and never send bank or ID details before a signed contract.

How the match score works

45% text similarity between your CV and the advert, 30% the share of the advert's tools and skills that your CV shows, 15% the role is a type you chose, and 10% the level suits a graduate. Adverts asking for more years than your limit, or for a language you did not list, are left out.

Tailoring

For each job you can see which of the advert's skills your CV already shows and which it does not. “Tailored CV” downloads a Word copy of your CV with a “Key skills for this role” line built only from skills already in your CV. The tool never adds anything you have not written. The command-line version can also reorder your projects and write a cover letter from a structured profile.

Privacy

Your CV and tracker never leave your browser. The Excel and Word files are built on your device with Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly).

Applying

The assistant does not submit applications for you. Each “Apply” button opens the employer's own application page. That keeps you in control of what is sent and respects each site's rules.
